Good to know

General medicine safety tips

This information is general guidance only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a licensed pharmacist or doctor for questions about your health or medication.

🕑

Check expiry dates

Never use medicines past their expiry date — potency and safety can no longer be guaranteed.

📋

Follow the label

Read dosage instructions carefully and keep medicines in their original packaging.

🧊

Store properly

Keep medicines in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and out of reach of children.

💬

Ask a professional

Speak to a pharmacist before combining medications or if you notice any side effects.
